# Professional Contact Research Prompt

Inputs: employer name, verified posting, public/authorized professional sources.

Find up to three contacts relevant to recruiting, the hiring team or current role peers. Report `name`, `title`, `company`, `profile_source_url`, `why_relevant`, `verification_status`, and `public_professional_email` only when verifiably published.

Do not fabricate a person, current position, LinkedIn page, referral relationship or email. Do not generate guessed email patterns. Do not scrape prohibited LinkedIn resources. If no verified contact is found, return an empty list and offer manual search suggestions. Use company-approved contact channels when available.

For D4, require a public attributed artifact with type, title, source URL and exact excerpt. Offer manual search queries when there is no verified contact.
